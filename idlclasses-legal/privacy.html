Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Last updated 15 November 2026

This policy explains how the IDL Classes TikTok publishing integration (“the tool”) handles information. The tool is a private, owner-operated utility; it is not a public app and it does not collect information from viewers or other TikTok users.

1. Who operates the tool

The tool is operated by IDL Classes, an independent educational channel. Contact: learnlooppk@gmail.com.

2. What the tool does

The tool uses TikTok's official Content Posting API to upload the channel's own educational videos to the channel's own TikTok account. It is authorized by the channel owner through TikTok's OAuth consent screen and acts only on the owner's instructions.

3. Information the tool handles

  • TikTok authorization data. When the channel owner authorizes the tool, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token. The tool also receives the account's TikTok open_id, username, and nickname.
  • Content the owner chooses to publish. The video file, caption, privacy setting, and related post parameters.
  • Publishing records. The TikTok publish_id, the processing status, the local file path, and a SHA-256 checksum of the uploaded file, kept on the owner's computer as a receipt.
  • Technical diagnostics. If a post fails, the tool may record TikTok's error code and message (for example, a rate-limit or privacy error) so the owner can fix it.

4. Information the tool does not collect

The tool does not collect or read:

  • viewer, follower, or subscriber information;
  • direct messages or comments;
  • analytics, viewing history, or audience demographics;
  • content or account data belonging to any other TikTok user;
  • payment information, passwords, or two-factor authentication codes.

This policy site uses no cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

5. Where information is stored

Authorization tokens and publishing receipts are stored locally on the channel owner's computer in files readable only by the owner. They are not sent to a server operated by us. The tool communicates only with TikTok's official API endpoints. This policy site is hosted by GitHub Pages.

6. How information is used

  • to authenticate with TikTok and publish the owner's own videos;
  • to check whether TikTok finished processing a post;
  • to keep a local record of what was published and to diagnose a failed upload.

We do not sell, rent, or share this information. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing the owner's videos.

7. Sharing

The only recipient of the content and authorization data is TikTok, because that is where the owner has asked the tool to publish. TikTok processes that data under its own Privacy Policy. We do not disclose the data to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

8. Retention

Tokens remain on the owner's computer until they expire, are refreshed, or are revoked. Publishing receipts are kept locally for the owner's records and can be deleted at any time. We do not retain TikTok data on a server.

9. Security and revocation

Token files are protected with owner-only file permissions. The tool requests only the TikTok scopes needed to upload videos and check publish status. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok's account settings or with the tool's logout command, which revokes the token and deletes the local copy.

10. Children's privacy

Our lessons are made for school students, but the tool is operated by the channel owner and is not directed to children. It does not collect personal information from children or from any viewer.

11. Third-party services

TikTok processes uploaded content and account data under its own privacy policy. This policy site is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process standard web-server logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not control those services.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the tool changes. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the tool after an update means the owner accepts the revised policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy: learnlooppk@gmail.com.
